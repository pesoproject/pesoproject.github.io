Stewarding the scientific software ecosystem

PESO exists to sustain and advance a robust, versatile, and portable HPC-AI software ecosystem and support the community of people who make that ecosystem effective. PESO sponsors the Software Ecosystem for Science (E4S) and the Spack package management system for open science. PESO contributes to community engagement through the Consortium for the Advancement of Scientific Software (CASS), the Better Scientific Software (BSSw) website and Fellowship program, and the High Performance Software Foundation (HPSF).
