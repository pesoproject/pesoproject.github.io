[Better Scientific Software (BSSw)](https://bssw.io) is a community hub for sharing best practices in scientific software development, and home to the Better Scientific Software Fellowship Program — a partnership among DOE ASCR, NNSA, and NSF.

- **BSSw.io:** A resource of articles, blog posts, and training materials covering better planning, development, performance, reliability, collaboration, and skills for scientific software — published over 40 new articles in FY2025 alone, with contributions from over 345 people.
- **BSSw Fellowship Program:** Recognizes individuals advancing developer productivity and software sustainability. The program has recognized 38 BSSw Fellows and 38 Honorable Mentions across cohorts from 2018–2025, with a 2026 cohort of 6 Fellows and 6 Honorable Mentions recently selected.
- **Established by IDEAS-ECP:** The Fellowship Program traces back to the IDEAS-ECP Project, carrying forward its mission of improving software productivity and sustainability practices.

**PESO's role in BSSw.** PESO leads the [BSSw.io](https://bssw.io) website and leads the [Better Scientific Software Fellowship Program](https://bssw.io/fellowship). PESO members also lead the CASS Workforce Working Group, which advances leadership and career paths in next-generation computing sciences, and coordinated recognition of Fellowship cohorts at US-RSE conferences through a memo of understanding with the US Research Software Engineer Association. Learn more at [bssw.io](https://bssw.io).
