The [Consortium for the Advancement of Scientific Software (CASS)](https://cass.community) coordinates DOE-funded software stewardship organizations (SSOs) — PESO, FASTMath, RAPIDS, S4PST, STEP, and CORSA — to advance scientific software in service of scientific discovery.

- **Ecosystem-level coordination:** CASS provides shared infrastructure, coordination, and stewardship practices that improve interoperability, quality, and adoption across a diverse software portfolio, complementing (not replacing) product-level stewardship by individual SSOs.
- **Working groups:** CASS working groups — including Software Ecosystem, Integration, Impact Framework, Software Quality Assurance, User-Developer Experience, and Workforce — bring member SSOs together on shared technical and community goals.
- **A loose federation with real structure:** Since bootstrapping in Fall 2023, CASS has established a charter, by-laws, a Steering Committee, and a public [software catalog and website](https://cass.community).

**PESO's role in CASS.** PESO leaders invested significant effort in bootstrapping CASS and continue to serve in its leadership roles:

- **David Bernholdt** (ORNL) — Chair of the CASS Steering Committee (FY25–26); CASS Impact Framework Working Group chair (FY25–26); CASS bootstrapping co-lead (FY24)
- **Terry Turton** (LANL) — Vice Chair of the CASS Steering Committee (FY25)
- **Jim Willenbring** (SNL) — Vice Chair of the CASS Steering Committee (FY26); CASS Integration Working Group chair (FY25–26)
- **Mike Heroux** (ParaTools) — CASS Software Ecosystem Working Group chair (FY25–26)
- **Lois Curfman McInnes** (ANL) — CASS Workforce Working Group chair (FY25–26); CASS bootstrapping co-lead (FY24)
- **Hannah Cohoon** (LBNL) — CASS User-Developer Experience Working Group co-chair (FY25–26)

Many of PESO's public-facing activities are CASS-branded, reflecting PESO's role at the ecosystem level and its structural connections to the other SSOs. Learn more at [cass.community](https://cass.community).
